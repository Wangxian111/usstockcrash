Method & Data

A backtest is a historical simulation, not live trading; only 5 ≥10% drawdowns — limited statistical significance

Scoring and Classification

All signals use only data available on or before the day; monthly data lags one month

Raw weighted score = Σ (signal state score × weight) ÷ 100
Weights: credit 25 · breadth 20 · valuation 15 · volatility 15 · macro 15 · rates 10
Rolling 3-year percentile = percentile of the current raw score across the past 756 trading days
  • Early warning: T-30 percentile ≥ 80, or T-10 percentile ≥ 80 with T-30 percentile ≥ 65.
  • Partial warning: T-30 percentile ≥ 65, or T-10 percentile ≥ 75, but not meeting the early-warning rule.
  • Coincident confirmation: within 10 trading days after the peak, the max percentile ≥ 80.
  • Missed: none of the above.
  • 8–10% drawdowns: excluded from these classifications and listed separately, so small declines aren't counted as “crises.”
  • Drawdown definition: SPY close ≥ 10% below its all-time high (main statistic) or 8–10% (listed separately).

Limitations

These constraints directly affect the “can it predict” answer

  • Proxy error: HYG/LQD is not HY OAS and RSP/SPY is not true breadth; credit and breadth signals lag or distort.
  • Missing signals: leverage/liquidity (NFCI), sentiment, and structural risk are excluded, so the model is less sensitive to liquidity shocks.
  • Small sample: 2017–2026 has only 5 ≥10% drawdowns; any hit rate could be luck.
  • Hindsight rules: the thresholds (80/65, etc.) were set after the backtest, so there is some overfitting risk; do not treat this as a live strategy.
  • Not a trading strategy: no transaction costs, slippage, or taxes are modeled, and no buy/sell recommendations are given.
