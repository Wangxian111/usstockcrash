Condition Detail

All use extended-window percentiles to avoid look-ahead

  • The 7 conditions in A/B: close <200-day MA, close <50-day MA, RSI(14) bottom 20%, weekly close <30-week MA, ATR top 20%, VIX top 20%, MACD histogram bottom 20%.
  • The 11 conditions in C: A/B plus weekly RSI bottom 20%, 21-day ROC bottom 20%, volume >1.5× 20-day average, drawdown-from-high bottom 20%.
  • Pure fundamentals are weaker: among credit/breadth/valuation/rates/macro, only the volatility (VIX) signal has a 10.9% hit rate (lift 3.27); high breadth and valuation percentiles are actually contrarian (lift 0.5–0.6). So all three variants are “trend first, volatility second.”
  • How to choose: pick A if you don't want frequent interruptions; B to catch every in-sample event; C for tactical risk control where extra false alarms are acceptable.
  • In-sample caveat: the three condition sets were screened on the full sample, so these are in-sample results; no independent out-of-sample feature selection was done — discount the hit rates.

Raising precision usually costs recall and later exits; decide which you want first

Other Attempts: Mostly No Improvement

Same 8% crash within 10 trading days definition

  • Credit-signal top-20% filter: hit rate 26.4%, but covers only 4/9 events — too strict.
  • VIX term-structure inversion: hit rate 15.7%, covers 7/9 — worse than the baseline.
  • 2/3/5-day persistence confirmation: hit rates 19.7% / 19.0% / 17.3% — no improvement, just delay.
  • Weekly Bollinger expansion top 20%: hit rate 22.1%, but covers only 3/9.
  • Within 2% of the high only: trend indicators barely light up; the only improvement is VIX top 20% (8.2% vs 1.1%, lift 7.5), but the sample is tiny — “leading but weak.”

The More Practical Approach: Tiered Warnings

Don't chase a single “perfect hit rate”; tier signals by their hit rate and coverage instead

  • Observation tier: B ≥4/7 — broad coverage, use it to start reviewing positions.
  • Warning tier: B ≥5/7 — balanced, use it to scale out / tighten risk controls.
  • Confirmation tier: B ≥5/7 + volume — higher hit rate but later trigger, use it for the final defensive actions.
  • Data and validation: only 9 events in 10 years is a tiny sample; real reliability gains require longer history (from 1990), independent out-of-sample tests, and event-level confidence intervals — not more conditions.

Method & Data

Technical indicators use only data available on or before the day; the target uses the next 126 trading days

Indicator Set

Daily + weekly, no parameter optimization, to avoid overfitting

  • Trend: close vs 20/50/200-day MAs, 50/200 death cross, weekly close vs 30-week MA.
  • Momentum: RSI(14), MACD(12,26,9), 21/63-day ROC, weekly RSI, weekly MACD, 4-week ROC.
  • Volatility: Bollinger band width and percentile, ATR/close and percentile, weekly Bollinger width percentile.
  • Volume: volume / 20-day average volume.
  • Candlestick patterns: bearish engulfing, shooting star, outside-down day, gap down (daily).
  • Target: max drawdown of SPY from the current close ≤ −10% within the next 126 trading days.
  • Sample: only trading days within 5% of the all-time high, so “already falling” isn't mistaken for “predicting a fall.”
  • Model: L2 logistic regression, class_weight=balanced; purged walk-forward with the last 190 training days dropped each year to avoid label overlap.
  • Warnings: within each test year, take the top 20% / 10% by predicted percentile for retrospective evaluation; live thresholds must come from the training set.

Limitations

These constraints mean the “hit rate” cannot be read as live returns

  • Small sample: about 1,573 near-high days, but very few independent events; labels overlap heavily.
  • Unstable by year: the pure technical model had a 0% hit rate in 2021 and 65.9% in 2025, with a median of only 20.4%.
  • Weak AUC: the pure technical model's AUC is 0.522, only slightly above random; the 42.9% top-10% hit rate may be driven by a few periods.
  • No gain from the overlay: the fundamental risk score barely lights up near highs (≥45 only 10 times, 0% hit rate), and adding it actually drags the pure technical model from 30.9% down to 27.0%.
  • Not a trading strategy: no transaction costs, slippage, or taxes are modeled, and no buy/sell recommendations are given.
