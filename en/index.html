Method & Risk Budget

Read the main layer first, then use the auxiliary score as background; when they disagree, follow the main layer's risk actions

How the Main Layer Works

7 conditions, extended-window percentiles, no look-ahead

Observation tier: ≥4 of 7 conditions triggered
Warning tier: ≥5 triggered
Confirmation tier: ≥5 triggered + volume >1.5× 20-day average
Percentiles: low-percentile conditions trigger in the bottom 20%, high-percentile conditions in the top 20%.
  • Trend: close <200-day MA, close <50-day MA, weekly close <30-week MA.
  • Momentum: RSI(14) bottom 20%, MACD histogram bottom 20%.
  • Volatility: ATR top 20%, VIX top 20%.
  • Confirmation: volume >1.5× 20-day average (a separate confirmation layer, not counted among the 7 conditions).

Historical Boundaries

These numbers determine what it can and cannot do

  • What it can do: after a decline has started, confirm trend damage and help with de-risking/tightening controls; variant B covered 9/9 in-sample episodes.
  • What it cannot do: predict tops. 0/9 triggered before the peak; at the median trigger ~6% had already been lost.
  • Small sample: 2017–2026 has only 9 episodes of 8% two-week drawdowns; the three combos are in-sample screens — discount the win rates.
  • Auxiliary score's role: valuation/credit/macro are slow variables; the current auxiliary score of 28.5 (Watch) disagrees with the main layer's 6/7 Warning, meaning the technical layer deteriorated first while fundamentals have not confirmed.

Risk Budget

Tiers are only reminders — position sizing decides survival

  • Any single high-volatility holding ≤ 5% of the portfolio.
  • Leveraged ETFs in total ≤ 10%, with a −20% stop preset.
  • Keep 6–12 months of living expenses in cash.
  • No margin-chasing; never invest money you need soon.
  • Positions that can't survive the drawdown have no bounce to look forward to.
